# JupiGuide — Digital Innovation Concept

**Group project · Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf · 2024**

JupiGuide was a product concept for a more interactive and personalized way to explore cities.

The idea was to go beyond conventional travel-planning apps by combining AI-supported recommendations with guided city experiences.

## Concept

The proposed application included:

- personalized recommendations based on user preferences
- generated or curated city tours
- information about nearby places and local businesses
- adaptable itineraries
- interactive maps and points of interest
- gamification elements
- optional augmented-reality concepts

## Product perspective

The project also considered:

- user problem and value proposition
- target groups
- competing alternatives
- MVP testing
- potential monetization through premium plans, advertising and partner recommendations

## Portfolio context

This was a conceptual digital-innovation project, not a production application. I include it to document experience with product ideation, problem/solution framing, MVP thinking and presenting a technology-enabled business concept.

# Brain-Machine Interfaces — Digital Innovation Seminar

**Individual seminar · Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf · 2023**

This seminar explored Brain-Machine Interfaces (BMIs) as a digital innovation at the intersection of computer science, neuroscience, signal processing and medical technology.

## Research question

How have Brain-Machine Interfaces developed, and what are their current applications, technical challenges and ethical implications?

## Topics covered

- historical development of BMIs
- neural-signal acquisition and processing
- EEG, ECoG and intracortical approaches
- invasive, non-invasive and hybrid BMIs
- decoding neural signals into machine commands
- prosthetic control and assistive applications
- restoration of sensory functions
- cochlear implants as a related application example
- technical and clinical challenges
- privacy, autonomy and ethical implications of neurotechnology
